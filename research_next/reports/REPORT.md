# research_next — Automated Crypto Income Study ($1,000 total capital)

Generated by `run_all.py` + `make_report.py`. All rules frozen before runs; no parameter tuning after seeing results. Cost stress = fee+slippage multiplier 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 on the 0.15%/side base.


## 1. Data & sources

- Spot: OKX USDT spot 1D candles, 12 majors, 2019-01 -> 2026-11-25 (`data/spot/`, fetched by slow_study/fetch_data.py).

- Perp funding: Binance USD-M `fundingRate` full history per symbol (`data/fetch_binance_funding.py`). OKX public funding history only serves ~3 months, so Binance is used; venue funding rates are correlated but NOT identical — documented approximation.

- Quarterly futures: Binance USD-M delivery klines (`data/fetch_quarterly_futures.py`). BTC contracts from ~2020, ETH from 2021-02 (ETH quarterly launched later — ETH B results start 2021).

- Limitations: perp marked at spot price (no alt perp OHLC history); funding marked at daily close; USD vs USDT basis ignored; liquidation checked on daily HIGH only (intraday path unknown); cross-venue basis (OKX spot vs Binance perp/futures) ignored.

## 5. Strategy A — multi-asset funding arb

Top-3 trailing-30d funding, weekly rebalance, alloc 0.90/K, negative-funding avoidance. Result: **does NOT beat BTC-only S5 1x**. Alt funding is lower on average, selection churn costs $200-350 over the period (vs $26 for S5), and the 0.90 alloc cap dilutes carry. At 1.5x/2.0x cost stress it loses money. L=2 min liq distance 1.9% — fragile.


## 6. Strategy B — cash-and-carry basis

Long spot + short quarterly future, hold to expiry, roll if basis >3%/yr. ETH basis was large (entries at 5-20%/yr annualized). **Best raw returns** but 8-9 futures-liquidations per run: with IMR=20% the short future leg is effectively 5x-leveraged and ETH/BTC rallied >20% within quarters repeatedly. Each liquidation loses the margin account (~16% of deployed capital). Sensitivity (reports/sensitivity_B.csv): ETH stays profitable across IMR 10-35% and basis thresholds 2-5%; BTC is marginal. Higher IMR = fewer liquidations but lower return on capital.


## 7. Strategy C — adaptive funding carry

BTC carry with weekly exposure multiplier from trailing funding + 30d vol (frozen thresholds). L=1: $1,134 final, 0.26%/mo, maxDD -0.6%, min liq dist 62%, zero liquidations, robust to 2x costs. **Positive and safe but BELOW S5 1x ($1,434)** — the exposure cuts (m=0.25/0.5 regimes) cost more carry than they save in risk, because S5 1x was never near liquidation anyway. L=2: $1,187, min liq dist 14% — not approved-leverage territory.


## 8. Liquidation & capital-utilization analysis

- A: 0 liquidations all configs; max util ~1.01-1.03 (reserve briefly negative-equivalent via margin top-ups — engine allows reserve floor 0).

- B: 8 (BTC) / 9 (ETH) liquidations at IMR=20%; util ~1.00 while in market (fully deployed spot+margin).

- C: 0 liquidations; util ~1.07-1.12 (L=2).

- S5 1x benchmark: 0 liquidations, min liq dist 47.3%.


## 9. Sensitivity

- Costs: see §3 — A goes negative at 1.5x; B degrades ~7%/step; C barely moves (few trades).

- B IMR/basis: see reports/sensitivity_B.csv — ETH robust, BTC marginal.

- A/C rules were frozen pre-run; no post-hoc tuning performed.


## 10. Ranking & recommendation

1. **B_cashcarry_ETH** — highest net return (CAGR 13.6%, ~1.15%/mo mean, 10.6% of months >=6%) but with 9 liquidation events and -13% maxDD. Deserves further testing ONLY with a safer margin structure (higher IMR, cross-margin with spot collateral, or smaller per-cycle allocation).

2. **S5 1x (existing benchmark)** — $1,434, 0.44%/mo, maxDD -0.9%, 47% liq distance. Still the best risk-adjusted carry.

3. **C_adaptive_L1** — $1,134, 0.26%/mo, safest (62% liq distance) but underperforms S5 1x; the adaptive overlay did not pay for itself.

4. **A_funding_arb** — rejected: multi-asset selection destroyed value vs BTC-only after costs.

5. BTC buy&hold — highest CAGR but -53% DD; not income, not comparable.


## 11. Final answer

**Given $1,000 total capital, the strongest empirically supported prospect of recurring monthly income is Strategy B cash-and-carry on ETH quarterly futures — IF the liquidation problem is fixed.** As modelled (IMR 20%, isolated margin) it earned ~1.15%/month mean with 10.6% of months >=6%, but suffered 9 liquidations and -13% maxDD; the edge is real (ETH basis premium) but the risk structure is not yet acceptable. The compromise required: run it at IMR >=35% (or cross-margin the spot leg as collateral), which cuts return on capital roughly in half but removes most liquidations (sensitivity: ETH IMR=35% still final $1,347 vs $1,652). If that compromise is unacceptable, **S5 1x remains the deployable answer**: ~0.44%/month, near-zero drawdown, 47% liquidation distance — modest but credible, and it is the only candidate that is both profitable after 2x costs and catastrophe-free in-sample. No strategy here reaches 6%/month sustainably.
